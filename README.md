HerRights AI is a college project prototype designed to help women understand their legal rights, improve cyber safety awareness, and find relevant support and reporting resources in one place.

The platform provides simplified information about women's legal rights, cyber safety, emergency support, and official reporting resources. It also includes an AI-based chat interface that provides general guidance based on the user's situation.

> **Note:** HerRights AI is an educational college prototype and does not replace professional legal advice.

---

## Problem Statement

Many women may not be aware of their legal rights, available support services, or the correct procedure for reporting incidents such as cybercrime, online financial fraud, domestic violence, and workplace harassment.

Information is often spread across different websites and may be difficult to understand or locate quickly.

HerRights AI aims to provide a simple and accessible platform where users can find legal awareness information, cyber safety guidance, emergency resources, and reporting links in one place.

---

## Proposed Solution

HerRights AI combines legal-rights awareness, cyber safety information, emergency resources, and an AI-assisted chat interface into a single web application.

Users can:

- Learn about women's legal rights.
- Access official Indian legal information through India Code.
- Learn basic cyber safety practices.
- Access the official National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
- Find important emergency and women-support numbers.
- Search emergency resources.
- Ask the HerRights AI assistant questions in natural language.
- Create an account and log in.
- Use the application without creating an account.

---

## Key Features

### 1. AI Legal Rights Assistant

The chat interface allows users to describe their situation in their own words and receive general guidance.

Topics include:

- Domestic violence
- Cybercrime
- Online financial fraud
- Workplace harassment
- Evidence preservation
- Women's legal rights

---

### 2. Rights & Resources

The Rights & Resources section provides access to:

- Women's legal rights information
- Indian legal resources
- India Code
- Cyber safety guidance
- Cybercrime reporting resources
- Emergency support information

---

### 3. Cyber Safety & Reporting

Users can learn basic practices such as:

- Never sharing passwords or OTPs.
- Avoiding suspicious links.
- Preserving screenshots and messages.
- Keeping usernames, URLs, and transaction details as evidence.
- Using the official cybercrime reporting portal for serious cybercrime.

The application provides access to:

**National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal:**  
https://cybercrime.gov.in/

**Cybercrime Helpline:**  
1930

---

### 4. Emergency Help

The Emergency Help section provides important emergency and women-support information across India.

The prototype includes important numbers such as:

- Emergency Response — 112
- Women Helpline — 181
- National Commission for Women — 14490
- Cybercrime Helpline — 1930

The application also provides state-wise women helpline information and a search option to help users find relevant information.

---

### 5. User Accounts

Users can create an account and log in to the application.

The prototype also supports using the application without creating an account.

The account interface includes:

- Sign up
- Login
- Profile icon
- Change account
- Use without an account
- Sign out
